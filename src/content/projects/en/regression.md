We built a full suite of automated regressions for Mercado Libre's checkout: more than 100
end-to-end scenarios over the critical purchase flows, payment method combinations and discount
combinations.

Playwright drives the user interactions and Cucumber describes each scenario in plain language, so
technical and non-technical teams read and write the same tests.

The result: far fewer bugs when shipping new features and in the checkout's weekly releases.
